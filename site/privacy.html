Privacy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Short version: your budget never leaves your device. There's no account, no sign-in, no tracking and no ads.

What the app stores

Your pay schedule, bills, set-asides, spending notes and whether you've unlocked the full version. All of it is saved in your browser's own storage on this device. AppNookStudio can't see it, and it isn't sent anywhere.

What we collect

  • No personal information, bank details or budget numbers.
  • No analytics, cookies, ad trackers or third-party scripts.
  • Your unlock code is checked inside the app on your device. It isn't sent to us.

The app's files are delivered by our web host, Cloudflare. Like any website, their servers may briefly log basic technical details such as your IP address to keep the service running and secure. We don't use those logs to track you.

Backups and exports

When you save a backup or export a spreadsheet, the file goes to your device. Where it goes after that, such as email or cloud storage, is up to you.

Deleting your data

Use More → Erase everything in the app, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Clearing browser data also deletes your budget, so save a backup first if you want to keep it.

A planning tool, not advice

This app helps you plan your own money. It isn't financial, tax or legal advice.
